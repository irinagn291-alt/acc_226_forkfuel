Camera permission is requested only when the reviewer opens Fuel Catalog and selects the Scan segment. The usage description string is exactly: ForkFuel reads barcodes to load food into your training-day plan. The app does not request photo library, location, microphone, contacts, health, Bluetooth or notification permissions. There is no login, no in-app purchase, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no user account and no account-deletion flow. Users can erase all local logs, plans, wishes and targets from Fuel Goals via Reset all data. ForkFuel is a personal food log for athletes who eat differently on Training days than on Rest days. It tracks energy and macros against separate Training and Rest targets, assigns food to Pre-Fuel, Refuel, Recovery or Top-Up slots, and plans ahead across 14 days. It is not medical advice. No credentials or demo account are needed. On a cold launch the app shows a brief loading state while it calls a registration endpoint. Within five seconds it opens the native interface unless a cached web URL is already stored or the registration response selects web content mode. First launch shows Ignition onboarding: swipe through three intro pages, optionally adjust Training targets on the fourth page, then tap Write targets or Skip. The main screen is Today's Fuel. Tap Find fuel to open Fuel Catalog. Use Search to type a product name or Scan to read a barcode (camera permission appears here). Select a product to open Portion, set grams, then Assign slot to open Park the Fuel and Confirm. From Today's Fuel, Forward Fuel opens the 14-day plan, Buy List opens saved products, Session Tags toggles Training or Rest for upcoming days, and Fuel Goals edits targets or opens Contact ForkFuel. Barcode scanning can also be tested without the camera by entering a manual code or tapping a shelf sample such as Rolled Oats. The app contacts world.openfoodfacts.org over HTTPS for product search at /api/v2/search and product lookup at /api/v2/product/{code}.json. At launch it also contacts https://forkfuel.pro/api/v1/users/register through NetworkService.performRegistration. The Contact ForkFuel sheet loads https://forkfuel.pro/contact-us. If registration returns a web content URL, that page may be shown instead of the native interface. Food logs, plans, buy-list items, macro targets, session tags and cached catalog entries are stored on device in SwiftData. Onboarding completion and optional cached registration data use UserDefaults. Behaviour does not differ by geographic region. Weekday versus weekend defaults for Training and Rest follow the device calendar only. No gambling, alcohol, tobacco, medical-device or other regulated-industry licence applies; the app is a self-contained nutrition log with a non-medical disclaimer and public Open Food Facts data.
