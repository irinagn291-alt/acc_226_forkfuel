ForkFuel is a personal food log for athletes who track energy and macros against separate Training and Rest targets. Product nutrition is looked up from Open Food Facts; bundled shelf samples stay available offline.

• Ignition onboarding introduces Training-day mode and writes Training targets (Rest targets are derived automatically)
• Today's Fuel shows energy and Protein, Carbs and Fat progress against the active day's budget
• Switch a day between Training and Rest, or browse previous and next days
• Session Tags pre-tags each of the next 14 days as Training or Rest
• Fuel Catalog with Search and Scan to find products by name or barcode
• Portion sets grams with a gram wheel and shows per 100 g and this-portion totals
• Park the Fuel assigns Pre-Fuel, Refuel, Recovery or Top-Up, eaten today or on a future day
• Forward Fuel lists food parked across the next 14 days; Eat now moves a planned item to today's eaten log
• Buy List saves products from Portion; Eat today or Plan ahead promotes them into the log or plan
• Fuel Goals edits Training and Rest targets, re-runs Ignition, resets all local data, or opens Contact ForkFuel
• Remove items from the eaten log with confirmation

ForkFuel is a personal food log, not medical advice.
